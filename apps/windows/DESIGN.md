# SpektraFilm Windows — visual language

The shell is a darkroom bench, not a catalog-style photo editor. Controls stay dense and labeled in plain words. The character is in the materials.

- **Bench.** The left column is the enlarger bench: numbered stations, a film-edge of sprocket holes, and a brass expose plate that stays pinned while the bench scrolls.
- **Easel.** The center is a black easel with a double mat and registration corners. An empty gate is drawn as an unexposed frame. While a render runs, a single safelight sweep crosses the gate.
- **Densitometer.** The right column is a readout log. Figures are monospaced. A cyan / magenta / yellow dye edge marks the spectral stack the simulator is built on.
- **Type.** Newsreader carries the wordmark, station titles, and the expose button. IBM Plex Mono carries indexes, values, and the log. Both are SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `src/fonts/OFL.txt`). Interface controls stay in the system sans.
- **Color.** Emulsion black and warm paper ink. Dye cyan, magenta, and yellow are accents, used once each as structure (status lamp, station index, dye edge), not as a neon theme.

Motion is the sweep during develop and a short fade when a print appears. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns both off. Focus is a yellow ring. Hint text stays light enough to read on the bench.
